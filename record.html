How to record
  1. Find a quiet room. Hold the phone about a hand's length from your mouth.
  2. Tap the orange button, wait half a second, say the phrase once, tap again to stop.
  3. Say it naturally, the way you'd really say it to Nik. Not too slow.
  4. Tap play to check. Not happy? Just record again.
  5. When you finish a week, tap Send to Nik at the bottom.

Your recordings stay on your phone until you send them. You can stop and come back any time.
